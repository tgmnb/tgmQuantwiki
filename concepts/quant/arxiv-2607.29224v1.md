## 摘要

A mass-preserving low-Mach-number framework is proposed for closed-system real-fluid flows governed by general nonlinear equations of state. The formulation enforces consistency between the spatially uniform thermodynamic pressure, the equation of state, and global mass conservation. Moreover, the numerical algorithm employs a segregated strategy in which the thermodynamic state is updated before the momentum equations, and the velocity field is advanced using a pressure-correction method.
