## 摘要

Compact web agents can reduce deployment cost, but training them poses challenges in both data collection and post-SFT reinforcement learning (RL). Successful trajectories are expensive to collect and often contain inefficient detours. After supervised fine-tuning (SFT), full trajectory corpora are dominated by routine states; moreover, when group-relative RL is applied to web actions, inadequately designed action-level rewards can yield weak or misleading relative updates, while groups rejected
