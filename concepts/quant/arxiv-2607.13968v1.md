## 摘要

Measuring sentiment from financial news is a central task in economics and finance, yet most existing indicators rely on dictionary-based approaches that infer sentiment from word counts and only partially capture context, negation, and semantic structure. This paper proposes a framework for constructing daily news mood indices using transformer-based language models and evaluates whether they better represent sentiment than dictionary-based alternatives.
