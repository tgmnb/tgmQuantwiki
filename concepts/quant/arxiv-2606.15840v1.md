## 摘要

We study a dynamic inspection problem for infrastructure systems in which multiple vehicles are routed and scheduled to monitor components subject to heterogeneous and stochastic failures. A key challenge is endogenous uncertainty in which failure-time realizations are filtered through prior inspection actions and failure-propagation dynamics.
