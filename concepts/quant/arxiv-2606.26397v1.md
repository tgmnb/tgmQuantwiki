## 摘要

Real-world decision-making often requires balancing multiple conflicting objectives, a challenge that standard Reinforcement Learning (RL) frequently addresses by aggregating rewards into a single scalar signal. While effective for simple tasks, this approach often fails to capture the full spectrum of optimal trade-offs, known as the Pareto frontier.
