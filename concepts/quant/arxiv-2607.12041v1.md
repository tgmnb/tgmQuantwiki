## 摘要

The structure of jets produced in high-energy nucleus-nucleus collisions carries information on parton energy loss and interaction in the quark-gluon plasma. This parton energy loss results in migration of jets in terms of transverse momentum, leading to a selection bias in inclusive jet measurements.
