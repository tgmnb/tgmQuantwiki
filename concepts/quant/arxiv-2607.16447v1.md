## 摘要

While non-intrusive reduced-order emulators successfully substitute for costly finite-element simulations, certification-grade applications demand a rigorous error assessment relative to the solver's own discretization uncertainty. This work introduces a verification framework designed to evaluate prediction accuracy directly against this numerical uncertainty floor, rather than physical observation.
