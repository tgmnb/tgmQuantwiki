## 摘要

The paper studies parametric classes of almost stochastic dominance on general Polish spaces as order relations for probability distributions with parameter gamma in [0,1]. Larger gamma gives weaker order relations: gamma=0 gives classical stochastic dominance, gamma=1 gives a complete preorder based on comparing expectations of a fixed increasing function g. Classical stochastic dominance X <=_st Y can be characterized by existence of a solution to an optimal transport problem with OT_c(X,Y)=0 for a suitable cost c. The paper generalizes this so the best possible gamma for almost stochastic dominance can be determined from the solution of a suitable optimal transport problem.
