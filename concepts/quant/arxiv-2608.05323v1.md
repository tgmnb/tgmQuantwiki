## 摘要

This search targets the resonant production of lepton-enriched semivisible jets (SVJs) from a strongly coupled dark sector, using 138 fb$^{-1}$ of proton-proton collision data collected with the CMS detector at the CERN LHC at $\sqrt{s}$ = 13 TeV. Two scenarios are investigated: jets enriched in all lepton flavors (SVJ$\ell$ signature) and jets predominantly enriched in tau leptons (SVJ$τ$ signature).
