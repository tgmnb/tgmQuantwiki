## 摘要

Density functional theory (DFT) serves as a reliable tool for atomistic molecular simulations, while machine learning potentials have become powerful complements to balance accuracy and efficiency. In this work, we release OpenGEM26 (Open Generated Ensemble of Molecules, 2026), a large-scale dataset comprising 200,000 unique molecules and 4.4 million conformations composed of H, C, N, O, S and Cl with up to ten heavy atoms. All calculations are carried out at the ωB97X-D/Def2-SVP and Def2-TZVP l
