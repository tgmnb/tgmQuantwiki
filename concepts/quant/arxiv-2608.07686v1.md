## 摘要

In the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA), the entanglement topology, where qubit pairs are connected by two-qubit gates, is conventionally set equal to the edge set of the problem graph. This coupling ties circuit design to explicit problem knowledge and may not yield the most trainable circuit under limited optimization budgets.
