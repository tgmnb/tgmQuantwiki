## 摘要

Using neural networks for stock return prediction typically requires choices about depth and hidden-layer width that are difficult to connect to financial interpretation. We study an alternative: estimate dependence among firm characteristics with a Maximally Filtered Clique Forest (MFCF), then map its clique structure to a Homological Neural Network (HNN).
