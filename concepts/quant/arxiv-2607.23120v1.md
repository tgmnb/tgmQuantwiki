## 摘要

Stock recommendation systems face the dual challenge of adapting to rapidly changing market conditions while maintaining low-latency predictions for end users. Traditional batch-trained models fail to capture concept drift, and monolithic architectures struggle to provide fault tolerance under load. This paper presents a scalable online deep learning-based stock recommendation system built on a distributed microservices architecture using Kubernetes, Docker, and RabbitMQ.
