## 摘要

Liability insurance for AI-powered legal services offers a promising solution to two critical barriers in using AI to expand access to justice: mitigating catastrophic risk to individual users from inadequate advice and ensuring meaningful accountability when failures occur. Existing accountability mechanisms face significant challenges: tort liability frameworks encounter barriers including judgment-proof providers and costly information asymmetries, while current regulatory approaches revolve
