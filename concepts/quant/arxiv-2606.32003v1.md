## 摘要

This paper presents a comparative study between Adaptive Quantized Control (AQC) and Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG) reinforcement learning for uncertain linear systems with input quantization over communication channels subject to packet loss. The considered setting also includes dynamic switching from a nominal unstable system to a more unstable one during operation.
