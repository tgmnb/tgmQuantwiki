## 摘要

Cosmic-ray muon scattering has shown considerable potential for detecting nuclear materials and other dense contraband, but practical deployment remains challenging. A major difficulty arises from the coupling between material properties and muon momentum, since the broad natural momentum distribution influences the scattering angle and prevents unambiguous material identification. In this work, we propose a Coarse Momentum-Aware Domain Adaptation (CMADA) method to enable precise identification
