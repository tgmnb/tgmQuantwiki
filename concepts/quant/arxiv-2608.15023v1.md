## 摘要

Extreme weather leaves power systems exposed to residual outage risk even after physical resilience investments. Parametric insurance can provide pre-agreed contingent liquidity, but its physical value depends on how trigger thresholds and payout levels are designed. This paper proposes a resilience oriented parametric insurance framework that couples a three tier wind-index contract with post-event network restoration.
