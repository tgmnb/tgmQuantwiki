## 摘要

Recently, test case selection (TCS) techniques have been explored to support the operational evaluation of deep neural networks (DNNs) under limited testing budgets, where labeling cost is a primary concern and uncovering model failures early is a key objective. Although prior studies report promising results, existing empirical evaluations focus almost exclusively on vision-based DNNs and datasets, leaving it unclear whether prior findings generalize to LLM code models.
