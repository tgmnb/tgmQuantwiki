## 摘要

Engine order enhancement is central in automotive sound design, where selective harmonics are synthesized to shape perceptual qualities such as sportiness, refinedness, or power. This paper investigates a neural network-based approach to combustion engine sound modeling that extends conventional engine order analysis and enhancement by deriving synthesis parameters from audio data with machine learning and incorporating stochastic components into the synthesis framework.
