## 摘要

Long-term time series forecasting (LTSF) plays a crucial role in fields such as energy management, finance, and traffic prediction. Transformer-based models have adopted patch-based strategies to capture long-range dependencies, but accurately modeling shape similarities across patches and variables remains challenging due to scale differences. To address this, we introduce patch-mean decoupling (PMD), which separates the trend and residual shape information by subtracting the mean of each patch
