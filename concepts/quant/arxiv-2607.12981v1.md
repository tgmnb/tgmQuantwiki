## 摘要

A central model in quantum machine learning is the quantum neural network (QNN), whose design requires balancing expressivity and trainability. Technically, expressivity is studied through circuit-function analysis, such as quantum signal processing, while trainability is analyzed using dynamical-Lie-algebra (DLA) methods.
