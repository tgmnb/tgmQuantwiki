## 摘要

This study investigates the efficient deployment of graph autoencoders, a class of graph neural networks (GNNs), for model-order reduction (MOR) of high-dimensional dynamical systems. The proposed framework leverages graph autoencoders to perform nonlinear dimensionality reduction, enabling low-dimensional representations of systems characterized by sharp gradients for which conventional linear approximations, such as proper orthogonal decomposition (POD), are inadequate.
