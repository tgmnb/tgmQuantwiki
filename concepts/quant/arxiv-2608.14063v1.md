## 摘要

Machine learning is rapidly reshaping constitutive modeling, offers new ways to learn material behavior directly from experimental data, and challenges long-established modeling paradigms. But with a growing number of machine-learning-based approaches available, how do they compare in practice? In this paper, we use the classic experimental data of Treloar to benchmark popular frameworks for hyperelasticity: (Generalized-Invariant) Constitutive Artificial Neural Networks, Physics-Augmented Neura
