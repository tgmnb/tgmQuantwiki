## 摘要

Reinforcement learning post-training dramatically improves LLM reasoning, but suffers from training instability and diversity collapse. Advantage functions offer an appealing fix: they reshape the training objective, reweight which rollouts drive learning, and are trivial to implement. Yet a proliferation of methods makes it unclear which advantage to use and when.
