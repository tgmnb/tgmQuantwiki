## 摘要

Vision-language models achieve strong general perception but often struggle with the spatial reasoning required for embodied tasks. We present RoboSpatialBrain, our submission to the RoboSpatial Challenge at the Embodied Reasoning in Action Workshop, CVPR 2026, built on RoboBrain2.5-8B-NV.
