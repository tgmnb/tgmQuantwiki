## Abstract

Existing work on AI openness has focused on defining what technical components or release practices qualify a system as "open". However, less is known about how openness is understood and put into practice by people who adopt and adapt these models under real-world constraints. In this paper, we present an empirical study of r/LocalLLaMA, a large online community centered on running and customizing open foundation models locally.
