## Abstract

Remaining useful life (RUL) estimates support reliability and maintenance decisions only if both point accuracy and prediction intervals remain trustworthy when operating conditions change. Convenient mixed splits can hide that failure. This paper studies the question on a documented 10-bearing PHME subset with time-varying load and speed.
