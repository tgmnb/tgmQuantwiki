## Abstract

The collective risk model is a fundamental framework in insurance ratemaking for modeling aggregate losses by combining claim frequency and claim severity components. A key structural requirement for a reliable experience rating system is a monotone ordering property: policyholders with worse past experience should receive a stochastically larger prediction for future losses, and hence a higher premium.
