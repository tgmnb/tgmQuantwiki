# Upper bound to optical forces through the multipolar control of optical beams

## Abstract

Optical tweezers enable the manipulation of microscopic objects using light, yet the fundamental limits to the optical forces that can be exerted on matter remain unknown. Here we derive a general upper bound to the maximum optical force that can be applied to a particle, based on an expansion of electromagnetic fields into well-defined helicity multipolar modes.
