## Abstract

Technology computer-aided design (TCAD) semiconductor device simulation is fundamentally constrained by the high computational cost of iteratively solving coupled drift-diffusion equations. Existing ML surrogates either reduce internal physics to macroscopic scalar regressions, or rely on single-step mappings that lack the iterative refinement required to resolve stiff, coupled fields.
