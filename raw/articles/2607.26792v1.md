## Abstract

Probabilistic K-line forecasting describes uncertainty in four complementary prices, namely open--high--low--close (OHLC). However, it introduces two consistency problems: quantile crossing and K-line crossing. Quantile crossing occurs when a higher-quantile forecast falls below a lower-quantile forecast, while K-line crossing occurs when the forecast low exceeds the open or close, or the forecast high falls below the open or close.
