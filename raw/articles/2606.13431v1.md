# Adaptive rerouting reshapes impacts of maritime chokepoint disruptions

## Abstract

Maritime chokepoints concentrate shipping traffic. Disruptions to this traffic can have a widespread impact on the global economy. However, the way in which these impacts are shaped by the shipping sector's adaptive behavior is not well understood. Here, we introduce an empirically calibrated full-scale agent-based model of the global commercial shipping fleet, representing 35,954 active ships moving among 1,651 ports.
