## Abstract

This paper reformulates the correction factor in the force-state of the bond-associated peridynamic (BAPD) model. The reformulation is established from the strain energy density equivalence between the BAPD model and the classical continuum mechanics (CCM) model at a material point. With the FEM solution taken as the reference, the proposed correction factor improves the accuracy of the BAPD solution in this study.
