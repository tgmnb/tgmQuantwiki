# Multi-Asset Liquidation in Dark Pools with Adverse Selection

**ArXiv ID:** [2607.27019v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27019v2)

## Abstract

We study the optimal liquidation of a multi-asset portfolio using both a traditional exchange and dark pools in the presence of quadratic adverse-selection costs. The problem leads to a matrix-valued backward stochastic differential equation with jumps and a singular terminal condition. We establish existence and uniqueness of its solution and use it to characterize the value function and the optimal liquidation strategy.
