## Abstract

A conservation-law-guided classification framework is developed for the execution of coarse-graining operations in quantum field theory. Coarse-graining produces a selection at the level of Feynman diagrams. Diagrams with nonzero momentum transfer are suppressed by oscillatory factors, zero-momentum-transfer ladder diagrams can accumulate through geometric series resummation to produce a spectral pole, and single-bubble topologies contribute only to the continuum.
