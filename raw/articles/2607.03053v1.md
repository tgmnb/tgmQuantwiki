## Abstract

This paper designs and develops a miniaturized photoacoustic spectroscopy gas probe with acoustic pressure enhancement for in-situ detection in oil-immersed power equipment. The probe adopts a single-cavity single-fiber structure, integrating the photoacoustic cell and the Fabry-Pérot optical sensing cavity within a ceramic ferrule with an outer diameter of only 800 um.
