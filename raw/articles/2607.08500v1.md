## Abstract

This paper proposes a stochastic discount factor (SDF) scaled by time-varying volatility. By utilizing prices and market data implied solely from S\&P 500 options, the proposed framework recovers a stable, non-monotonic SDF that captures the pure forward-looking expectations of market participants while mitigating observation noise.
