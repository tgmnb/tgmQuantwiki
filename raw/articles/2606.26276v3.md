## Abstract

While human factors in the privacy of fertility tracking apps -- health trackers that record users' menstrual or pregnancy data -- has been the subject of extensive study, little attention has been paid to the technical aspects of apps' data handling practices. We conduct a network-based measurement study of a corpus of 20 Android fertility tracking apps from the Google Play Store, focusing on how user data is shared with third party advertising services.
