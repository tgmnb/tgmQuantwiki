# Signature-Based Optimal Execution for Statistical Arbitrage with Path-Dependent Trading Signals

## Abstract

We develop a signature-based framework for optimal execution in statistical arbitrage strategies with path-dependent predictive signals. Both the alpha process and the trading speed are modelled as linear functionals of the truncated signature of a time-augmented market path, placing signal generation and execution on the same truncated signature basis.
