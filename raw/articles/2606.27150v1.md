## Abstract

We study endogenous reinsurance pricing in a competitive insurance market with one strategic reinsurer and many heterogeneous insurers. The reinsurer acts as a Stackelberg leader by choosing a common premium rate and an investment strategy, while insurers decide how much risk to retain and how to invest, taking into account their own performance, their performance relative to the insurer population, and common insurance-claim and financial-market noise.
