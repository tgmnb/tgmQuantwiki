## Abstract

We study the squared price-of-risk premium of a portfolio -- an integrated conditional squared Sharpe-ratio functional, not an expected excess return -- and its attribution to causal drivers. Relative to a declared admissible benchmark it decomposes into intervention-stable premium, a signed causal distortion (the confounding wedge), and a nonnegative information loss; the loss is an $L^2$ projection residual, the wedge is not.
