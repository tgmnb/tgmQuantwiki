## Abstract

Sectoral default dependence is usually described by a static correlation matrix, a static copula, or a small number of common factors. Such representations, when specified separately at each observation horizon, do not by themselves explain why the effective dependence observed in monthly credit data differs from that observed after annual aggregation. This paper proposes a dynamic low-rank state-space model for monthly multi-sector default-count data and studies the dependence structure induced
