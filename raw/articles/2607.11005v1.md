# Actor-Critic Learning for Extended Mean Field Control with Deterministic Policies

**ArXiv ID:** [2607.11005v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11005v1)

## Abstract

This paper develops a model-free reinforcement learning framework for continuous--time extended mean field control problems, where both the dynamics and reward may depend on the joint distribution of states and controls. We adopt deterministic feedback policies, under which the state--action distribution is induced directly as a push--forward of the state law.
