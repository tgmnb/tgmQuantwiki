## Abstract

This paper presents a unified dataset and modeling framework for financial alternative dispute resolution (ADR) cases collected from multiple Japanese ADR organizations. Each case consists of paired claims from the complainant and the respondent with a binary settlement outcome.   We introduce a functional tagging scheme to represent dispute structures and propose a multi-task model that jointly performs dispute classification and settlement prediction.
