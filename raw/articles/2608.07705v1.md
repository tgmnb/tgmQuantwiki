## Abstract

Clinical foundation models trained on large-scale patient data are increasingly used for decision support, screening, and public health. As deployment expands, privacy risk increasingly arises from model-mediated leakage, yet its prevalence and severity remain poorly quantified. Models can disclose sensitive training artifacts, enabling patient re-identification in ways not captured by data-handling controls alone.
