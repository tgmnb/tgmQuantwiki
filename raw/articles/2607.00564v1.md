## Abstract

The knowledge of isospin-breaking effects in hadronic $τ$ decays is required for a high-precision determination of the Hadronic-Vacuum-Polarization contribution to $(g-2)_μ$ from experimental $τ$ data. In this work we present a strategy for their calculation in a fully inclusive setup from first-principles Lattice QCD+QED simulations.
