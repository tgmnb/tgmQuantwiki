## Abstract

Solving linear differential equations is a fundamental task in scientific computing and an important primitive for quantum computing. A recent one-ancilla quantum differential equation solver provides a hardware-friendly and locality-preserving approach with provable performance guarantees, making it highly suitable for the early fault-tolerant and near-term regimes.
