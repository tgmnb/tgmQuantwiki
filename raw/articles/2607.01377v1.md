## Abstract

We estimate Kyle's (1985) price-impact coefficient $λ$ directly from daily equity order flow and test its ability to forecast the cross-section of subsequent stock returns. Using CRSP data from 2020 to 2025, we construct firm-month measures of signed order flow and two estimators of $\hatλ_{it}$: a within-month price-impact regression and an Amihud-style ratio.
