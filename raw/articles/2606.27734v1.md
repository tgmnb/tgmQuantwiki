Fault-tolerant quantum algorithms offer a promising pathway for estimating the ground-state energies of periodic materials that are beyond the practical reach of classical electronic-structure methods. A remaining challenge is finite-size mitigation: quantum algorithms evaluate a finite supercell or finite Brillouin-zone mesh, while materials properties are defined in the thermodynamic limit.
