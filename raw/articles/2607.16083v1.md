## Abstract

The growing adoption of Large Language Models in scientific research has created a need to understand what competencies researchers and graduate students require to use these tools critically and responsibly. This rapid review analyzed 194 articles retrieved from Elicit and Google Scholar (2022 to 2025), from which 40 were selected for competency extraction and thematic analysis following independent dual screening (Gwet AC1: 0.76 to 0.83).
