## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) is rapidly becoming a defining feature of contemporary labor markets, yet it remains unclear whether its diffusion is producing a common set of competencies across occupations or deepening occupational divisions. We investigate how AI related skill demand is reshaping labor market structure using large scale online vacancy data from ten countries spanning the Global North and Global South.
