## Abstract

This paper extends the cap-axis integral diagnostic to general characteristic axes and measures factor-model pricing errors as bridge-alpha curves. A predetermined characteristic order generates prefix portfolios; subtracting equal-exposure aggregate portfolios gives zero-investment bridges indexed by cutoff p.
