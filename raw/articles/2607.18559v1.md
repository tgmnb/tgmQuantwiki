# Mixing-Free and Signal-Optimal Learning of Gaussian Graphical Models from Glauber Dynamics

**ArXiv ID:** [2607.18559v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18559v1)

## Abstract

Gaussian graphical model selection is usually studied under independent sampling, but in many applications the data arise as a single trajectory of a dependent stochastic process. We study exact recovery of the graph from one trajectory of random-scan Gaussian Glauber dynamics. Existing techniques for this problem either inherit the mixing time of the chain, which can be super-polynomial in the dimension $p$ without strong assumptions, or are suboptimal in the minimum normalized edge strength $κ
