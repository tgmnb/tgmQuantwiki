## Abstract

With the rapid rise of large language models, LLM-driven quantitative factor mining has become an increasingly active research area. However, existing methods still suffer from subjective direction design, limited integration of up-to-date multi-source information, semantic drift, factor redundancy, and the absence of an end-to-end feedback loop from factor discovery to portfolio backtesting.
