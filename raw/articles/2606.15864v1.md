## Abstract

Specific heat treatments applied to superconducting radiofrequency (SRF) cavities, such as nitrogen infusion or Mid T baking, aim to improve the quality factor (Q0) at medium accelerating fields (10 to 20 MV/m). These treatments reduce the BCS surface resistance by tuning the mean free path of niobium over a few hundred nanometers, either by diffusing oxygen from the native oxide layer or by diffusing nitrogen after the dissolution of the oxide layer.
