## Abstract

This paper discusses some aspects related to gradient-based optimization algorithms with special focus on the requirements associated to their use in the implementation of Nonlinear Model Predictive Control. Based on a dedicated discussion, a new algorithm, termed Search and Accelerate (SaA) is proposed that mixes together a novel line search, a trust region mechanism together with an adaptation of the gradient acceleration scheme.
