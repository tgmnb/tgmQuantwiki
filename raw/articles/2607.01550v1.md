## Abstract

Systematic trend following has, on average, been profitable for at least two centuries; yet since approximately 2009, short-term trends have ceased to deliver reliable returns. Using a cross-section of roughly 100 liquid futures contracts spanning 1995-2025, together with an industry-representative CTA proxy, we document the break and characterise its dependence on signal speed and asset class.
