## Abstract

The distribution of neutral hydrogen (HI) in the post-reionization universe traces the cosmic large-scale structure and therefore serves as a powerful cosmological probe. An efficient way to measure its distribution over wide sky areas and redshift ranges is through single-dish intensity mapping, which exploits the autocorrelation signal of each dish in a telescope array while scanning the same sky patch.
