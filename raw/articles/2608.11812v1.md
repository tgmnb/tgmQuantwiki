## Abstract

Foundation machine-learned force fields (MLFFs) are often pretrained on broad materials datasets whose electronic-structure conventions may not reproduce the phase energetics required for a specific correlated material. Using NiO as a case study, we examine whether incorrect source-level phase energetics can be corrected efficiently through target-level fine-tuning.
