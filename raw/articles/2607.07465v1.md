## Abstract

Markowitz defined portfolio risk as an internal property, built from the covariance among a book's own holdings rather than the distance to any index. Seventy years of simplification reversed that. The market beta of CAPM, the fixed style and industry axes of Barra-type models, and the promotion of benchmark deviation to the definition of risk all traded the inward view for an external one.
