## Abstract

We study a deterministic contagion model for a large population of financial institutions connected by a weighted directed exposure matrix. The sign convention and loss term are motivated by a default cascade with partial recovery and interest servicing, whereas the dynamic model records distress through occupation time and therefore permits recovery.
