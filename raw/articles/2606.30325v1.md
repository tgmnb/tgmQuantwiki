## Abstract

In this paper we investigate the stability properties of a fundamental mechanism of biological cells called phosphorylation. The system is a chemical reaction network (CRN) for which a chemical species, {\em the substrate}, can be sequentially transformed into two phosphorylated forms, by the activity of two types of enzymes, one type for phosphorylation, the other for dephosphorylation.
