## Abstract

This paper proposes a novel asset pricing framework that augments large language model (LLM) embeddings of annual report disclosures with supply chain knowledge graph (KG) propagation. Using FinBERT embeddings of 10-K MD&A sections for 255 S&P 500 firms over 2011-2025, two sets of return predictors are constructed: direct LLM embeddings and network-augmented embeddings, where firm-level signals propagate through inter-firm linkages.
