# GRASP: Reinforcing Language Model Anonymizers with Group Relative Policy Optimization

**ArXiv ID:** [2608.06526v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06526v1)

## Abstract

Large language models can infer sensitive personal attributes, such as age, location, and occupation, from ordinary text, turning everyday writing into a privacy risk. Adversarial anonymization defends against this by rewriting a text with a capable language model that also plays the attacker, but it needs a powerful model at inference time and thus sends private text to a third party, the very exposure anonymization should prevent. Recent work distills this behavior into a small on-device model
