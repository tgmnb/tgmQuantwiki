## Abstract

Harmful pesticide effects exceeding specific protection goals (SPG) may go undetected in underpowered experimental designs. Regulatory honeybee field studies have consistently failed to reach the statistical power required under European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) guidance, which may have caused approval of high-risk substances.
