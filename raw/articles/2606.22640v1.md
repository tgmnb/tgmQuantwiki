## Abstract

A phase-field-based reaction-diffusion corrosion model is developed to predict microbially influenced corrosion (MIC) in metal alloys, with a focus on anaerobic conditions and sulfate-reducing bacteria (SRB). The formulation couples microbial sulfate reduction, sulfate transport, electrochemical kinetics, material dissolution, and mechanical effects.
