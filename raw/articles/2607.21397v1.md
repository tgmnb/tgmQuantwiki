# Wilson Towers as Local Bulk Fields

## Abstract

Multi-winding Wilson loops (``Wilson spools'') in 2+1-dimensional gravity can reproduce one-loop partition functions of local free fields in the bulk. Bulk free fields have a second-quantized Fock space, and the AdS/CFT correspondence suggests that the associated multi-particle sectors are dual to multi-trace primaries in the CFT.
