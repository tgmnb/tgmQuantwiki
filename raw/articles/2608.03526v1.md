## Abstract

We study bounded finite-interaction-range operators $H$ on $\ell^2(Γ)$, where $Γ$ is a uniformly discrete subset of a left-invariant doubling metric measure space $(X,d,μ)$. Our goal is to approximate spectral information of $H$ from finite sections $H_{L,x}$ supported on balls $B_L(x)\capΓ$. The main technical input is a commutator estimate for Lipschitz ''tent'' localisations $W_{L,x}$, which depends only on geometric properties (doubling) and a uniform interaction degree.
