The development of nanotherapeutics often involves extensive empirical optimization due to the sensitivity of nanoparticle properties, such as size and polydispersity index (PDI), to minor changes in process parameters. Factors like formulation concentration, flow rates, and mixing ratios can significantly influence clinical efficacy and therapeutic outcomes.
