# How Well Can Your Video Model Remember? Measuring Memory-Budget Trade-offs in Long Video Understanding

**ArXiv ID:** [2606.20726v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20726v1)

## Abstract

We introduce a compact empirical model that quantifies how answer accuracy degrades as a function of frame budget B and temporal distance D in long video understanding -- analyzing performance when recalling content from D seconds in the past using a fraction B of total frames. Long-form models operate under strict budgets, yet no prior framework predicts how accuracy degrades as B shrinks and events recede.
