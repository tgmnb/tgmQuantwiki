## Abstract

Reduced-order models compress high-dimensional dynamics into low-dimensional representations that can be evaluated rapidly, but they lose accuracy when online dynamics drift beyond the training data. Adaptive methods address this by updating the subspace online with external, out-of-span information, such as full-order corrections or sensor snapshots.
