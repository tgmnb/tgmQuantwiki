## Abstract

In this paper we demonstrate how the Integrated Finite Element Neural Network (IFENN) framework can effectively model the entire evolution of phase-field fracture, including the initiation and propagation stage, across generalizable geometries. IFENN is a hybrid scheme for coupled computational mechanics problems, tightly coupling a standard FEM solver (mechanical equilibrium) with a pre-trained neural network (coupled field).
