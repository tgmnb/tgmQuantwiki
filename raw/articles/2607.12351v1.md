## Abstract

This paper presents a residual-certified adaptive method for tracking local solution manifolds in parametric dynamical systems. The method combines local POD reduction, full physical residual checks, state-distance snapshot forgetting, high-fidelity resampling, and a lightweight physics-informed neural correction.
