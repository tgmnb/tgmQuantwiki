## Abstract

Federated learning lets institutions train shared models without moving their data, which makes it a natural fit for health and life sciences research under strict privacy regulation. The methods are maturing fast, but the practical barrier now comes earlier: a team starting a federated project meets a scattered mix of frameworks, governance obligations, and unfamiliar roles, with no structured place to begin that fits its own background.
