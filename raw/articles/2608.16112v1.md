## Abstract

Technical debt is treated almost universally as an engineering pathology. This paper argues that under the conditions defining early-stage software work (high hypothesis uncertainty, cheap experiments, and the freedom to abandon), deliberately incurred technical debt is a rationally priced financial instrument: a call option on the validated product, purchased at a discount that is largest exactly when uncertainty is highest.
