# R^3: Advertisement Compliance Rectification via Group-Relative Experience Extractor and Curriculum Reinforcement

**ArXiv ID:** [2607.07318v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07318v1)
**PDF:** [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.07318v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.07318v1)
**Published:** 2026-07-08

**Authors:** 

## Abstract

Rigorous content moderation is crucial for online advertising but leads to millions of daily rejections. This scale renders manual rectification infeasible, particularly for video advertisements. However, existing safety-driven methods often suffer from aggressive over-editing, which compromises the advertiser's original semantic intent merely to satisfy compliance. In this work, we target the rectification of textual violations in video ads, covering both speech transcripts and on-screen text.
