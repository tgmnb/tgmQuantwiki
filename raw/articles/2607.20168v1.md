## Abstract

Do quantum kernels improve cross-sectional stock return prediction? We run a controlled horse race on the Chinese A-share market in which a quantum fidelity kernel, a projected quantum kernel, and a classical RBF control share identical training subsamples, solver, and tuning budgets, so that only the kernel is exchanged.
