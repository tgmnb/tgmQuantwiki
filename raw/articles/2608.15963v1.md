## Abstract

Demonstrating quantum advantage in a manner that can be independently verified by classical means remains one of the most pressing open problems in quantum computing. Here we propose a new heuristic approach for constructing quantum circuits that are hard to classically simulate yet whose output distributions can be efficiently verified classically.
