# LeNEPA: No-Augmentation Next-Latent Prediction for Time-Series Representation Learning

**ArXiv ID:** [2607.00958v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00958v1)
**PDF:** [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.00958v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.00958v1)
**Published:** 2026-07-01
**Categories:** cs.LG, cs.LG
**Authors:** 

## Abstract

Time series are central to modern data mining applications, from industrial telemetry and server metrics to finance and physiology, yet time-series self-supervised learning often depends on view and augmentation choices that encode domain-specific invariances. We study how an SSL recipe behaves when its method-specific configuration is reused unchanged after the pretraining signal family changes, framing this as a fixed-recipe stress test rather than a comparison against optimally tuned methods.
