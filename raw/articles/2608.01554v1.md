# P-Bubbles, Q-Bubbles, and Risk Premia

**ArXiv ID:** [2608.01554v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01554v1)
**PDF:** [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.01554v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.01554v1)
**Published:** 2026-08-03
**Categories:** q-fin.MF, econ.TH, q-fin.MF
**Authors:** 

## Abstract

We develop a unified modeling framework that connects two distinct types of bubbles defined in the literature: the rational bubbles (aka P-bubbles), and the local martingale bubbles (aka Q-bubbles). We show that the local martingale bubble model includes the classical rational bubble as a special case. We relate both types of bubbles to an equity's risk premium via a novel decomposition.
