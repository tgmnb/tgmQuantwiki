## Abstract

This paper presents a historical and technical survey of the hardware architectures, interconnection networks, and synchronization primitives that have shaped massively parallel systems over the past four decades. We examine the design of the NYU Ultracomputer and the IBM Research Parallel Processor Prototype (RP3), focusing on the hardware implementation of the Fetch-and-Add primitive in multistage interconnection networks.
