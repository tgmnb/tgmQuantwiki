## Abstract

This work investigates the potential benefits and technical challenges of using high-bandwidth flash (HBF) for large language model (LLM) inference. HBF has gained increasing attention as a promising solution to mitigate memory-capacity bottlenecks in modern LLM-serving systems, but its benefits and challenges remain largely uninvestigated.
