## Abstract

The surge of GPU-intensive workloads in artificial intelligence (AI) data centers drives massive energy demands, leading to soaring costs and significant stress on local power distribution networks. Coordinating delay-tolerant workload scheduling with power grid conditions via precise workload prediction can mitigate these issues.
