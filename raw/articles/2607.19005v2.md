## Abstract

The Observable Matrix Dynamics (OMD) approach monitors the time development of complex non-linear systems through the trajectory of a fixed-size distance matrix and its spectrum. We apply it to the S\&P 500 cross section over three crisis decades, the 2001 dot-com bust, the 2007--2008 financial crisis, and the 2020 Covid crash, with three fixed-size observables on a fixed universe.
