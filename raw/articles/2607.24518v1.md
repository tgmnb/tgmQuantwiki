## Abstract

Symmetric non-negative matrix factorization (SymNMF) recovers latent group structure from a dependence matrix, but its dense, quadratic-memory objective has confined prior work to moderate sizes. We present a large-scale GPU study of seven algorithm families (over 30 configurations) on absolute Pearson correlation and tail pairwise dependence matrices from Extreme Value Theory, two proxies for empirical risk-factor estimation on large portfolios.
