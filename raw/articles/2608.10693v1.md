## Abstract

Our primary goal is to forecast and empirically examine the evolution of the implied volatility (IV) surface, with particular focus on the dates of scheduled meetings of the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC). Firstly, we check if IV increases before the announcement and if thes effect is stronger for short-dated, out-the-money (OTM) options in high volatility regimes.
