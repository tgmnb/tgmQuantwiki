## Abstract

We present a physics-informed, data-free neural solver for partial differential equations, built on a graph neural network architecture that utilises message passing. By relying on the weak form of the problem, we use gradients of finite-element shape functions (which are therefore polynomials) rather than automatic differentiation operators to compute the residuals of the equation from the displacements predicted by the network itself.
