# From Financial Sentiment Classification to Return Predictability: A QLoRA Benchmark of Large Language Models

**ArXiv ID:** [2608.04200v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04200v1)
**PDF:** [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.04200v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.04200v1)
**Published:** 2026-08-04
**Categories:** q-fin.MF, cs.LG, q-fin.MF
**Authors:** 

## Abstract

Financial sentiment classifiers are commonly evaluated against human labels, but strong linguistic performance does not necessarily imply economically useful return predictability. This study separates these questions through two experiments. First, we construct a unified three-class benchmark from five financial text datasets and compare TF--IDF Naive Bayes, off-the-shelf FinBERT and Financial-RoBERTa encoders, zero-shot Qwen2.5-7B, and QLoRA-adapted Qwen2.5-7B, LLaMA3-8B, and Mistral-7B models
