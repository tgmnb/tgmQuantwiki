# A Computable Stochastic Riccati Equations Framework for Mean--Variance Portfolio Selection with Multifactor Stochastic Volatility Model

## Abstract

We investigate a computable and empirically implementable framework for continuous-time mean--variance optimal portfolio selection with random market coefficients. The market model is built on a tractable multifactor stochastic volatility structure, which captures state-dependent risk premia, stochastic volatility, and dynamic cross-asset dependence.
