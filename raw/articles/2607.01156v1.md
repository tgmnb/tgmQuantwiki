## Abstract

We present the verification of a thermo--hydrologic--mechanical capability implemented within the PFLOTRAN framework, with emphasis on benchmark-based assessment of the THM implementation. The thermal--hydrologic (TH) equations for mass and energy balance are solved on control-volume blocks or Voronoi cells, while the quasi-static momentum balance is solved on an element-based dual mesh.
