## Abstract

Creator economy platforms face a strategic dilemma: allocating traffic to established stars for immediate ad revenue versus nurturing emerging creators to build a follower base for future monetization. We develop a continuous-time dynamic optimization model to characterize the optimal traffic allocation policy for a platform managing heterogeneous creators with dual revenue streams (advertising and direct follower contributions).
