# Comparative Analysis of Low-Rank Adaptation in Large Language Models versus Dense Embedding Regression for Headline Click-Through Rate Prediction

## Abstract

Optimizing digital content headlines for click-through rate (CTR) is an important problem in online media and recommendation systems. While large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated strong generative capabilities, their effectiveness for discriminative ranking tasks, such as selecting the highest-performing headline from a set of candidates, remains less well understood.
