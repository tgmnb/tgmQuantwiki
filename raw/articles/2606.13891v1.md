## Abstract

Autonomous robotic systems, including autonomous vehicles, drones, and mobile robots, increasingly rely on on-device Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) to adapt to dynamic environments. Unlike cloud-based solutions, embedded DRL must perform training and inference directly on resource-constrained hardware while maintaining timely decision-making.
