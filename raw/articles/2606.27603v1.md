## Abstract

Quadrotors offer the agility needed to rapidly transport suspended payloads during time-critical applications, including search-and-rescue and medical delivery. While suspended-payload transport and traversal for these missions are well studied, the highly dynamic targeted release of the payload remains comparatively underexplored.
