## Abstract

Local-in-time well-posedness is established for a recently proposed diffuse interface model describing incompressible two-phase flows. The result constitutes the first analytical study of a model introduced by ten Eikelder et al. for the motion of a binary mixture of macroscopically immiscible, viscous, incompressible fluids with unmatched densities.
