## Abstract

This paper studies public-private partnerships that delegate access-to-credit programs to private equity and venture-capital intermediaries. The public sector seeks to relax credit rationing and expand lending to socially valuable firms, while delegated monitors screen applicants, allocate subsidized loans, and bear agency costs.
