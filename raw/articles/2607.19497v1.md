# The Science and Practice of Trend-Following Systems

**ArXiv ID:** [2607.19497v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19497v1)
**PDF:** [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.19497v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.19497v1)
**Published:** 2026-07-21
**Categories:** q-fin.ST, q-fin.MF, q-fin.ST

## Abstract

We present a unified approach to designing trend-following (TF) systems and classify them into European, American, and Time Series Momentum categories. For European TF systems, we derive an exact relationship between profit-and-loss, autocorrelation, and drift in volatility-normalized returns. We analyze the expected return under fractional ARFIMA processes and show that TF systems are profitable when the long-term autocorrelation is positive, even under short-term mean reversion.
